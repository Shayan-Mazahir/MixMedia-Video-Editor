# Settings

[← Help home](README.md)

## Appearance

**Edit → Settings… → Appearance**: **Dark**, **Light**, or **Match the system** (it follows your computer when that switches between light and dark). Changes show straight away; Cancel puts it back.

## Project shape and size

The **Shape** menu under the preview, or **File → Project settings…** for everything:

- **Shape**: *Auto* (same as the first video), 16:9 widescreen, 9:16 tall (Shorts, TikTok, Reels), 1:1 square, 4:5 portrait, 4:3 or 21:9 cinema.
- **Size**: 720p up to 4K.
- **Frame rate**: same as the first video, or 24 / 25 / 30 / 50 / 60.

Clips that are a different shape get black bars. To fill the frame instead, select the clip and tick **Fill the frame** under *Crop & rotate*. These get saved with the project.

## Performance

**Edit → Settings…** → Performance. Normally MixMedia works out by itself how much of your computer to use. Tick **Let me choose** to decide yourself:

- **CPU threads**: how many it may use at once for the preview, effects, exporting and auto-captions. Fewer keeps the rest of the computer snappier (handy while exporting, or on battery), but things take longer.
- **Use the graphics card for exporting** / **for auto-captions**.

These are for this computer, not saved in projects.

## Auto-save

While you have unsaved changes, MixMedia keeps a backup copy every minute in its own folder. Your project file is only ever changed when you save. If MixMedia crashes or the computer turns off, next time you open it you'll be asked whether you want your work back. Say yes, then save it.

## Updates

The first time you open MixMedia, it asks whether you'd like to hear about new versions:

- **New releases**: the normal ones.
- **Releases and pre-releases**: test versions too (newest features, maybe a bit rough).
- **Don't check**: MixMedia never asks GitHub about anything.

When it's on, MixMedia checks when you open it, at most once a day. It never interrupts you while you're editing. A brand-new release is only mentioned once it's been out for an hour, so its downloads are ready. When there's one, you'll see what's new and can **Download** it (opens its page in your browser), wait until **Later**, or **Skip this version**.

**Help → Check for updates…** (or **Check now** in **Edit → Settings… → Updates**) checks straight away, any time. Change your choice in **Edit → Settings… → Updates**.

The only thing sent is the question "which versions are there?" to GitHub. Nothing about you or your videos.
