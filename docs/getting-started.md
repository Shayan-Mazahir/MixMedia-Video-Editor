# Getting started

[← Help home](README.md)

Here's the whole trip, from a pile of files to a finished video.

When MixMedia opens, the welcome screen lets you start a **new project**, **open** one, **import** some files, or click a **recent project** to carry on where you left off. (Untick "Show this when MixMedia starts" if you'd rather go straight in; **File → Open recent** has the same list.)

## 1. Bring your files in

Click **Import** at the top left (or **File → Import media…**, `Ctrl+I`), or just drag files from your file manager onto MixMedia. When the **Media** tab is empty, clicking it works too. Your files show up there as cards, with how long each one is and whether it's a video, sound or a picture. Videos, pictures and sound files all work.

## 2. Put them on the timeline

Drag a file from **Media** down onto the timeline at the bottom, or double-click it to add it to the end. The first video you add decides the size and shape of your project (you can change that, see [Settings](settings.md)).

## 3. Cut it down

- Drag a clip to move it.
- Drag either **edge** of a clip to trim it.
- Put the playhead (the red line) where you want a cut and press **S** to split.
- Select a bit you don't want and press **Delete**. The gap closes up by itself.

More in [The timeline](timeline.md).

## 4. Make it nice

Click any clip and its settings appear in **Properties** on the right. The tabs on the left have ready-made **Titles**, **Effects**, **Transitions** and **Subtitles**: drag them onto the timeline or double-click to add them at the playhead.

## 5. Watch it

Press **Space** to play and pause. The arrow keys step a frame at a time; **↑** and **↓** jump between cuts.

## 6. Save your project

**File → Save project** (`Ctrl+S`) saves a `.mixmedia` file you can open again later and keep editing. MixMedia also keeps a backup every minute, in case it crashes (see [Settings](settings.md)).

## 7. Export

Click the big **Export** button at the top right, pick a preset (YouTube, TikTok, GIF, ...) and go. See [Exporting](export.md).

## Your workspace

MixMedia opens at a size that suits your screen, and after that it remembers how you left it: the window's size and the panel layout.

- **Resize the panels** by dragging the gaps between them (library, preview, properties and timeline).
- **View → Show the library** and **Show properties** hide or bring back the side panels. **Focus on the preview** hides both for a bigger picture.
- **View → Full screen** (`F11`) for the whole screen.
- **View → Reset the layout** puts everything back to normal.

On a small screen or a narrow window, the toolbar buttons drop their words and just show icons (hover over one to see what it does), and the side panels slim down so the preview keeps its room.
