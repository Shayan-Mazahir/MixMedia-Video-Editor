# Titles

[← Help home](README.md)

## Adding one

Open the **Titles** tab on the left and drag a style onto the timeline, or double-click it to add it at the playhead. **Ctrl+T** adds a plain one. Titles live on the FX tracks, above your video.

## Changing it

Select the title and look in Properties under **Text**:

- Type the words in the box at the top (press Enter for a new line).
- **Font**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Size** and **Colour**.
- **Line up** left, centre or right, then **Left / right** and **Up / down** to place it.
- **Letter spacing**: spread the letters out (or squash them).
- **Outline**: a line around every letter, in any colour.
- **Shadow**: a soft shadow. Set its distance to 0 and give it a bright colour for a **glow**.
- **Box behind it**: a box in any colour, see-through or not.

Colour buttons with a "%" let you choose how see-through the colour is.

## Making it move

Under **Animation**, pick how it **comes in** and **goes out** (fade, slide, zoom, wipe, rise...) and how long that takes. Titles can use [keyframes](keyframes.md) too.

## Tip

The ready-made styles (Neon glow, Caption box, Cinematic...) are just titles with these settings filled in. Start from one and change it.
