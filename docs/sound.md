# Sound

[← Help home](README.md)

Select a clip and look under **Sound** in Properties.

- **Volume**: 100% is as recorded, up to 400%. It can change over time with [keyframes](keyframes.md) (the ◆ next to it).
- **Even out volume**: listens to the clip and sets the volume so it's nicely loud without distorting. Good for quiet recordings.
- **Remove noise**: takes out steady background noise: hum, hiss, fans, air conditioning. Start around 50% and go up until the noise is gone but voices still sound natural. Too high and voices start sounding underwater.
- **Duck under talking**: for music. It turns itself down while someone's talking on other clips, and back up afterwards. **Duck by** sets how far down.
- **Detach audio**: puts the clip's sound on its own track.

## Fades

Under **Fade**, set how long the clip fades **in** and **out**. That fades the picture and the sound together. Fade in a music clip over a couple of seconds for a gentle start.

## Speed and pitch

Sped-up or slowed-down clips keep their normal pitch unless you untick **Keep the sound's pitch** (under Speed).

## Transitions

When two clips have a transition between them, their sound crossfades automatically.
