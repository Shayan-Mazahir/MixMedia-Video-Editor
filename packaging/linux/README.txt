MixMedia Video Editor
=====================

To start it, double-click "mixmedia" in this folder, or run this in a terminal:

    ./mixmedia

Everything it needs is inside this folder, so you can put it anywhere. Your
settings, speech models and extra fonts go in "MixMedia data" in here too
(see "What's in here.txt" inside it).
To uninstall it, just delete the folder: that removes everything.

Using the AppImage instead? Give it a folder of its own: its "MixMedia data"
goes right next to it.
